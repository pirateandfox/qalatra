## Highlights

**Complete or skip tasks and meetings where you're working, with an optional note.**

- The task detail panel and meeting view now have Complete, plus Skip for recurring items.
  Recurring event cards get a Skip button next to "Mark done".
- The ▾ next to Complete opens a note box. The note is saved to the task's notes thread and
  to the agent record, the same place `complete_task` writes. The `complete` endpoint accepts
  an optional `{ note }`; calls without a body behave as before.
- Completing a task with open subtasks, or a meeting with unchecked agenda items, asks first.

**Habits get a year view, and agents can audit a whole year cheaply.**

- ▦ on any habit opens a year heatmap with completion %, current streak and best streak.
  Click a week or month to see those days and their notes, and switch years with ‹ ›.
- `GET /api/v1/habits/:id/history?start=&end=` returns stats and per-day rows.
- `get_habit_history` accepts `start`/`end`, `summary` (`week` or `month`) and
  `include_logs`. Called without them, its output is unchanged. In range mode
  `completion_rate` is a number (`93`) rather than the old `"57%"` string.

**@mentions in daily notes.**

- Type `@` to pick a task, project or context. Results are grouped, keyboard and mouse
  both work, and the mention is inserted as plain text.

**More keyboard shortcuts.**

- `e` edits the selected task's title, `⌘⌫` archives the selected task, and `⌘[` / `⌘←`
  goes back: it closes the meeting or detail panel, then returns to previously visited sections.
- None of them fire while you're typing in a field or the terminal.

**A tidier Tools tab that keeps your place.**

- The toolbar shows a connection status dot instead of the proxy address and session time.
  Those are in its tooltip. Loading lasts until the tool has actually rendered, and the
  toolbar collapses on narrow windows.
- Reconnect now reopens the page you were on, on desktop as well as mobile. The proxy
  reports the embedded page's location to the app. This needs the Qalatra server that hosts
  the tool, usually the remote box, to be on 1.9.58.
