## Highlights

**Answers given directly in Claude now reach FlightDesk.**

- Session-state reports forward Claude Bridge 0.1.18's resolved-approval history
  (`resolvedApprovals`, its cursor, and the truncation flag) unchanged. FlightDesk can then
  reconcile every page of an approval card, including ones answered in Claude rather than
  through FlightDesk.
- FlightDesk deduplicates entries itself, so Qalatra requests the bridge's default window and
  sends no cursor. Bridge read errors are not forwarded.
- With a bridge older than 0.1.18 the fields are simply absent and reports are unchanged.
  Reconciliation needs the matching FlightDesk support; Qalatra only relays the history.
